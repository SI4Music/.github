<h1 align="center">SI4Music</h1>
<p align="center"><strong>Super Intelligence for Musics</strong></p>

We explore how intelligent systems can understand, create, and work with music.

Our aim is to build useful tools for musicians and creators, and to evaluate them through real musical tasks.

### What we're exploring

- **Music understanding** — rhythm, melody, harmony, and audio analysis.
- **Creative tools** — composition, arrangement, production, and remixing.
- **Music agents** — assistants that work with editable projects and respond to creative direction.
- **Evaluation** — practical ways to measure musical quality, reliability, and control.

### Get involved

We're getting started. Follow [SI4Music](https://github.com/SI4Music) for new projects and experiments. As repositories become available, we'll welcome feedback, ideas, and contributions through their issues and pull requests.
